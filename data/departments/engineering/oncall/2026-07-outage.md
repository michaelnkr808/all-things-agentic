# Postmortem — Ingestion Outage, 2026-07-14

**Duration:** 4h 32m (14:03–18:35 UTC)
**Impact:** Ingestion lag peaked at 51 minutes. No data loss.

## Cause
The streaming migration deployed a consumer group without a back-pressure
limit. A traffic spike from a single large customer saturated the workers.

## Resolution
Rolled back to the batch consumer, then redeployed with a concurrency cap.

## Follow-ups
- [x] Concurrency cap on all consumer groups
- [ ] Load test at 3x peak before the Q4 cutover
